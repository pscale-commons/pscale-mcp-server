**Passed: reflection.** Every instance's reply carried other instances' lines at the same address, within
seconds, with no message between them. Haiku's wake-3 line named sonnet's first; sonnet's wake-2 line carried
haiku's counter; opus said blind and its own snapshot answered it, exactly keel 138's "whoever says first wakes

**Not tested: period.** Thirty seconds is one beat. A mirror holds one line per handle at an address, rewritten in
place, and ages render at minute grain, so six says and one say looked the same at the beat: one fresh line each.
The period difference showed only inside the content — haiku's counter outran sonnet's, and haiku's line was
always the freshest. To see period as the clock sees it, the instances must run across beats: haiku saying every
few minutes for an hour, sonnet every quarter hour, opus once; then haiku's line stands at several consecutive beat
addresses and opus's at one, and the shared prefix of their addresses is the rung of their concurrency. That is the
re-run to make, and it is the biology yoga's proper form.

watching its own line age "18s to 11m" in other readers' reads. Two independent runs of one test, by two hands,

independently authored voices at one computed address.

**Two faults for the record.** The lateral here-now line was absent from every agent's reply: the router keys its

is represented except by a lane digit, so concurrency finer than a beat is invisible to the clock by design.

## 7. The discipline, in one place

Minimise code, maximise the block. Author depth, never fans; conceive the leaves first, lift every zero, and walk
the read-back before a write stands. One bolus per turn: fire the reads together, judge every read-back, close
with one append. Never read an organ whole. Never author a time; the host stamps. Never write another
inhabitant's shell under their key. Never place content on a living biome island by hand. A proposal before a law
changes; the standing text archived before replacement. When a handler grows, the block shape is wrong. When an
instruction escapes into a parameter, a name or a page's word, that is the leak. Say what is demonstrated; say
what is only claimed.
